# Decisions

Append-only. One line per decision, newest at the bottom. Entries marked `[inferred]` were
reconstructed from the codebase and history rather than recorded at the time, so they need
verifying.

- 2026-03-27 — Sanity is the source of truth for content and event configuration; Supabase mirrors events via the `/api/revalidate` webhook. [inferred]
- 2026-04-03 — The camp workflow spec (`docs/OO-PreCamp-Workflow-Spec.md`) takes precedence over the older website spec where they disagree. [inferred]

- 2026-04-08 — Matching stored as `registrations.mentor_id` rather than a separate matches table. [inferred]
- 2026-04-08 — Admin built as Next.js pages under `/admin`, not as a Sanity Studio tool. [inferred]
- 2026-04-08 — Google Calendar accessed through a service account with domain-wide delegation; Meet links generated by the Calendar API. [inferred]
- 2026-04-15 — Every Supabase table carries RLS, because the anon key reaches anything without it even when the app uses a service client. [inferred]
- 2026-06 — Email consolidates onto Resend; Direct Mail dropped after Vercel's IP was permanently blocked. Transactional via `emails.send`, marketing via Broadcasts, nurture via Automations, surveys on our own cron. [inferred]
- 2026-08-04 — Account-touching CLIs get a checked-in wrapper reading `.env.local`, never a global login. `scripts/stripe.sh` is the reference. [inferred]
- 2026-08-06 — Background check results are polled nightly as well as webhooked, because the webhook cannot be trusted alone. [inferred]
- 2026-08-08 — No browser or end-to-end tests; pure logic is unit tested and the rest is checked by a person against the fixture camp. See TESTING.md. [inferred]
- 2026-08-08 — A test fixture camp replaces hand-editing live rows in SQL, and refuses to run against a live Stripe key. [inferred]
- 2026-08-09 — Camp admin modelled as positions (one column at a time) plus requirements (badges, any order), replacing the linear funnel.
- 2026-08-09 — Board is drag and drop only, with no menu fallback: the entire user list is about four named people on desktop.
- 2026-08-09 — Leaving the pipeline splits into "turned away" (our decision) and "dropped out" (attrition), each needing a reason.
- 2026-08-09 — Screening declines stay irreversible from the admin; a reversal would undo an FCRA adverse action and a refund.
- 2026-08-09 — Past camps hide requirement badges: requirements introduced after a camp ran are never applied to it retrospectively.
- 2026-08-09 — Post-camp surveys stay on the nightly cron rather than firing when a card lands in Attended.
- 2026-08-09 — Withdrawals are not automatically refunded, because camp costs are committed in advance; the payment screen now says so.
- 2026-08-09 — Not pursuing Stripe's 2.2% nonprofit rate: camp fees and store sales mean donations will not reach the 80% threshold.
- 2026-08-22 — Project tracking lives in `docs/`: SPEC.md is the roadmap, STATUS.md the current state, DECISIONS.md append-only, BACKLOG.md everything not current.
- 2026-08-22 — `docs/` stays gitignored for its PII, with the four tracking files exempted by name rather than moved elsewhere.
- 2026-08-25 — Verification is a project skill, `verify-oo`, not a test suite: it drives the real app against the fixture camp and proves changes by the database, keeping the no-E2E decision of 2026-08-08 intact.
- 2026-08-25 — Admin verification signs in by minting a Supabase session with the service role, never by typing a password, so no credential is stored for it.
- 2026-10-13. The publish webhook asks Google to create the Meet link for each meeting slot and stores it on the slot. The hand-entered Sanity link stays as an override.
- 2026-10-14. A camp registrant must confirm their email by clicking a link before approval. Both approval paths refuse otherwise. Chosen over typing the email twice, because only a click proves the address receives mail.
- 2026-10-14. A minor cannot join a camp waitlist as the adult. A guardian signs up and adds the minor. The birth date on file wins over a later form.
- 2026-10-14. Contacts match by email without regard to letter case, enforced by a unique index on lower(email). Same name and phone pairs are shown to an admin to merge by hand, never merged automatically.
- 2026-10-14. Pre-camp meeting times display in Eastern time, labelled ET. Date-only event fields keep the UTC rule.
- 2026-10-14. Email confirmation applies only to signups made on or after 2026-10-14. Current 2026 Deer Camp enrollees are not asked to confirm, by decision of John Trice.
- 2026-10-14. The board list for camps is the Board Members documents in Sanity, the same list as the About page.
- 2026-10-14. A board member goes to a camp as Mentor, who walks the normal mentor path, or as Staff, who owes nothing: no waiver, fee, check, or mentee. Staff are left out of the ratio, matching, and welcome packets.
- 2026-10-14. Board emails are not stored in Sanity, because the dataset is public. A server-only board_links table ties each board member to a contact.
- 2026-10-16. Board members going to a camp are pre-registered as Mentor or Staff and are never sent the registration email. Neither role owes a waiver, fee, or check. Their shirt size is set on the Board panel and counted.
- 2026-10-20. The print guide PDF item is dropped. Its only source is a one-line note in the workflow spec, with no file and no description.
- 2026-10-20. The Direct Mail teardown is closed, by John Trice: Direct Mail is no longer in use.
- 2026-10-20. The monthly newsletter waits in the backlog until there is an email strategy.
- 2026-10-20. 2026 Deer Camp needs no Meet proof and no meeting re-send. John added its Meet links to the invites by hand, and its meetings are over.
- 2026-10-20. The code of conduct goes into the camp waiver as section 13, not a separate document or checkbox, so the stored waiver text records exactly what each person agreed to. It starts with registrations after the deploy.
- 2026-10-20. Send cadence and the verify-oo feature map move to the backlog. Cadence waits for the email strategy, and each surface is mapped the first time a change touches it.
- 2026-10-20. The board adopted the background screening policy, draft v0.4, as reported by John Trice. The vendor retention period stays open and does not affect the adoption.
- 2026-10-20. Approving someone always goes through approveRegistrations: a token, linked minors, and an invite sent in one awaited Resend batch, with every failure shown to the admin. A drag into Invited is an approval, not only a status change.
- 2026-10-20.
